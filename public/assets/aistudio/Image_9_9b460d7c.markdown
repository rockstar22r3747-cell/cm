## Brand & Style

CoastMitra embodies a high-end travel app aesthetic, blending the tranquility of the ocean with the warmth of coastal destinations. The brand personality is trustworthy, inviting, and premium, evoking a sense of effortless exploration and direct, zero-brokerage connection. The visual style leans towards a modern, clean minimalism infused with sophisticated tonal accents and a dark mode environment to create an immersive, vacation-ready digital experience.

## Colors

The color palette anchors the user experience in deep tonal ocean hues for primary elements, complemented by a sophisticated dark background that immediately communicates premium hospitality and comfort. Muted secondary and tertiary accents draw the eye to key conversion points and calls to action, ensuring an intuitive and engaging user journey in a dark-mode optimized layout.

## Typography

Typography is crisp, modern, and highly legible across all devices. Utilizing a geometric sans-serif with soft, welcoming characteristics, the type system establishes a clear hierarchy that guides users naturally from immersive destination headlines down to fine-print booking details.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout relies on a fluid grid system that adapts seamlessly from compact mobile viewports to expansive desktop displays. Generous white space and consistent padding reinforce the high-end travel aesthetic, allowing high-resolution imagery and destination cards to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is communicated through subtle, multi-layered tonal surfaces and ambient, low-opacity shadows tinted with the primary tonal hue. This creates a floating, tactile card hierarchy that lifts interactive elements gracefully off the dark background without feeling heavy or cluttered.

## Shapes

The design system employs a rounded shape language to mirror the organic, smooth forms found in coastal environments. Cards, buttons, and input fields feature generous border radii, fostering an approachable, friendly, and polished user experience.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary actions utilize the vibrant tonal accent with generous padding and fully rounded or soft pill shapes. Secondary actions feature deep tonal outlines or subtle tonal fills.
- **Cards:** Destination and property cards feature rounded corners, subtle ambient shadows, and edge-to-edge imagery with overlaid gradient scrims for maximum legibility.
- **Input Fields:** Clean text fields set against contrasting surfaces with soft borders, featuring clear floating labels and instant validation states.
- **Chips & Tags:** Pill-shaped filters for amenities and travel styles that use soft tonal backgrounds and deep typography.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Customized with primary tonal accents and smooth state transitions.
